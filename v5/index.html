Part three

So, what's actually going on?

Here's the honest version. Your brain wasn't built to help you finish a portfolio, learn a language, or start a business. It was built to keep you safe and keep your energy for right now. Anything uncertain, effortful, or slow to pay off gets quietly flagged as "maybe not today." That's not a flaw in you. It's an old system doing exactly what it was designed to do.

The tricky part is that it rarely says "avoid this." It offers you something else instead — a quick hit of something pleasant, or something that genuinely sparks your curiosity. And because that curiosity is real, it doesn't feel like avoidance. Reading about your goal, reorganising your setup, falling into a fascinating side topic… it all feels close enough to progress that you'd never think to question it.

That's why willpower speeches don't really help. You're not choosing laziness over effort — you're choosing something that feels good and reasonable over something that feels uncomfortable and uncertain. Anyone would. The detour is designed to look like the road.

But here's the reframe: the moment you can say "oh — this is the detour," it loses a little of its grip. Noticing isn't a consolation prize. It's the first real step. You just took it by reading this far.
